Selling costs

Between 4.4% and 10.5% of every sale never reaches your wallet. Sales tax is always paid; the broker's fee only when you list an order — selling into someone else's buy order costs no broker fee. On a thin margin this is the entire margin, so it comes off every profit figure in the tool. Defaults assume untrained skills and no standings, which understates profit rather than flattering it.

Invention

A T2 blueprint is not free. It comes out of an invention job that burns datacores and fails more often than it succeeds — about 50% at max skills with no decryptor, so a successful BPC costs roughly two attempts. That cost is charged to every T2 row, divided by the runs on the resulting BPC. It matters most for T2 ships, which get 1 run per BPC where a module gets 10, so a hull carries ten times the share a module does.
